Build the candidate cognitive reasoning prova at `/candidato/prova-cognitiva/:candidaturaId` — the opt-in (`vaga.aplica_cognitivo`) mobile-first screen on the established SJT `ScreenShell`, presenting one CC0 reasoning item at a time with light proctoring (soft count-up timer, tab-blur/visibilitychange logging, paste-block), submitting ONLY raw picks to the `pontuar_cognitivo` RPC (server re-scores anti-tampering), and showing the candidate a neutral acknowledgment — never a score, band, threshold, or pass/fail (RNF-07a).

The candidate posts only raw picks; the server is the sole scorer; the gabarito never reaches the client. Product language stays non-psychological (LGPD-04).

Output: the `src/features/avaliacao-cognitiva/` feature (service/hook/screen) + the candidate route + a vitest opt-in/proctoring test + a Playwright e2e covering the opt-in gate both ways.
