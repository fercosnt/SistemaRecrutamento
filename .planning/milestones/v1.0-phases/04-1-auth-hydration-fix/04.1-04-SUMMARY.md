## Decisions Made

- **Path A (delete shim) chosen over Path B (rewrite REQUIREMENTS text):** Phase 4.1 RESEARCH §Open Questions Q1 recommended Path A on blast-radius grounds. Confirmed by Plan 02 SUMMARY consumer count (only 2 real importers of the shim).
